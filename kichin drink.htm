Transportation

【public transport】
Get off at JR Shimizu Station or Shizutetsu Shimizu Station. Take the Shizutetsu Bus Miho Yamanote Line bound for "Tokai University Miho Aquarium/Miho Garage-mae" and get off at "Hatoba Verkehr Museum." It is a 1-minute walk from there.

【car】
From the Tomei Expressway Shimizu Interchange, take Shimizu Marine Road for about 10 minutes.
